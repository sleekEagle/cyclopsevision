Blur is a measurement

A lens is sharp at only one distance. Everything nearer or farther blurs, and the amount of blur depends on how far it is from that distance. Most cameras treat blur as a defect. We use it to measure depth.

Move the focus distance and watch the blur on each object change. A model that has learned this relationship, on a camera built to make it strong, can turn one image into a depth map.

Video interpretability

Explain the when, not just the where

Image saliency maps tell you which pixels mattered. Video models also reason over time, so we trace a prediction back to the regions and moments that caused it.

In medical video (endoscopy, ultrasound, surgical footage) a clinician needs to see the evidence behind a model's output before relying on it.
