02 — Video interpretability

See what video models see, and when.

Video models now help with diagnosis, surgery and monitoring, but their decisions are spread across thousands of frames. We build methods that trace each prediction back to the regions and moments that caused it.

Collaborate with us Our approach →
The problem

Image saliency isn't enough

Running an image explainer on each frame produces flickering heatmaps and says nothing about time. A video model may depend on motion, ordering or one brief event, and an explanation needs to show that.

Spatial attribution

Which regions in each frame drove the prediction.

Temporal attribution

Which frames and segments mattered, and which the model ignored.

Event-level concepts

Explanations in terms people already use, such as "instrument contact" or "valve closure", rather than raw pixels.

What we focus on

Explanations you can act on

Faithful

Explanations that reflect what the model actually computes, checked with quantitative faithfulness tests.

Temporally coherent

Attributions that stay consistent from frame to frame, without flicker.

Human-readable

Results expressed in a domain expert's terms, so a clinician can check a model's reasoning.

Applications

Built for high-stakes video

Endoscopy

Show the frames and regions behind lesion or polyp detections.

Ultrasound & echo

Connect predictions to specific parts of the cardiac cycle and anatomy.

Surgical video

Audit phase recognition and skill-assessment models event by event.

Model debugging

Find shortcuts, spurious cues and dataset bias before deployment.
